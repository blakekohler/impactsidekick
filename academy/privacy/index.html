Privacy, in plain words

What we keep when you take Sidekick Academy, why, and how to get it back or delete it. Last updated November 4, 2026.

Please don't paste private details

Hoopaa is an AI. Don't paste names or details of clients, patients, residents, students or donors. Use placeholders like "Client A" or "a donor who gave $500." You'll get the same help with none of the risk.

What we collect

  • Your name, email, organization, role and staff size
  • The lesson work you submit and your conversations with Hoopaa
  • Your readiness assessment answers and results
  • Your XP, level, progress and certificate

Why

To run the course, give you feedback, issue your certificate, and make the lessons better. Marc and Blake at Impact Sidekick read conversations to see where people get stuck. If your assessment shows your organization could use help, Marc may email you once about your results.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information.
  • We don't show you ads.
  • Hoopaa runs on Anthropic's AI models through their business service, which doesn't use your conversations to train their models.

Who can see what

Teammates who join with the same organization see your name and level on your team board. The public leaderboard shows you only if you turn it on. Your certificate page is public only if you share its link.

How long we keep it

Hoopaa conversations are kept for 12 months, then deleted. You can download or delete everything at any time from your account page, or by emailing marc@impactsidekick.com.

The Sidekick Brief

If you tick "Also send me The Sidekick Brief," you'll confirm it with the newsletter's own sign-up form. You can unsubscribe from any issue.

Questions

Email marc@impactsidekick.com. A person reads every message.
