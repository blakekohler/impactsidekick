Disagree Better: five design directions and what's costing you donors

You have about 44,000 social followers and no email signup anywhere on the site, and your donate page scores 31 out of 100 on mobile speed. Fixing those two carries the most money and reach.

Next comes free reach you aren't using: no page-one Google ranking for four searches that fit their mission (one peaks at Thanksgiving, Nov 26), no sign of Google Ad Grants, and an unrated Charity Navigator listing with no mission or website on file.

Design directions

Five redesigns of the homepage hero and donate page. Each opens on the homepage; use Donate in its header to see the donate page.

1. The SeamBlue and red halves split by a line visitors can drag. 2. ConversationA respectful exchange in chat bubbles; donor questions answered as a thread. 3. PosterGiant overlapping type; where blue meets red becomes the page's ink. 4. Common GroundDark and cinematic; the two halves slide together as you scroll. 5. Bridge BuildersIllustrated; a bridge fills in as the gift amount grows.
