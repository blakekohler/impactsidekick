Part 1. AI for your routine writing

Each of these produces a draft in your voice that a staff member reads, edits if needed and sends. Nothing goes out on its own, and none of it needs student or participant records.

How these are built: a small tool connected only to what it needs (your donor system, your site's posts and events, or a meeting recording), trained on 5 to 10 examples of your past writing, delivering drafts to whoever owns that task.

1Donor thank-yous and tax receipts

Gifts reach you through the Neon CRM form on your donate page, Venmo, mailed checks and Live PC Give PC, and receipts today mean emailing Kim. Each new gift would produce a personal thank-you that names what the gift supports (Thrive, Same Inside, Sages & Seekers) and includes your receipt language and EIN, ready for Kim or you to approve in one click. Year-end and match-campaign gifts would get their own versions.

Uses
Read-only access to new gifts in Neon (or a daily export), plus a handful of your best past thank-you notes.
Setup
About 1 to 2 weeks (our estimate), with one working session with Kim.
Question for you
Your email signup runs on Little Green Light and your online donations on Neon. Which one is your donor record of truth?

2Board reports

A one-page board update drafted monthly or before each board meeting: gifts and totals against goals, program sessions and youth reached, events and volunteer turnout, and the decisions or asks coming up. With 16 board members, a consistent page saves the back-and-forth of individual updates.

Uses
Neon gift totals, your program sign-up counts and your events calendar. We agree on the 8 to 10 numbers that matter.
Setup
About 2 weeks (our estimate). After that, a draft lands a few days before each meeting.

3Meeting write-ups

Staff, board and committee meetings (including gala planning) recorded in Zoom or Google Meet turn into a short summary: decisions, who owns what, and due dates. It's emailed to attendees the same day, and follow-up reminders go out on those dates.

Uses
The meeting recording or transcript you already get from Zoom or Meet.
Setup
A few days (our estimate).

4The monthly newsletter, drafted for you

Your newsletter archive shows a steady monthly issue. A first draft would assemble itself from that month's Parent Corner posts, upcoming events, open volunteer spots and program news, in your format, ready for edits and scheduling.

Uses
Your blog and events pages, plus three past issues as the template.
Setup
About 1 week (our estimate).

5Event and volunteer listings

Roughly 95 listings this year, each with a description, venue, time and a volunteer ask. A short form (what, where, when, how many volunteers) would produce the website listing, a social post and a reminder email to signed-up volunteers.

Uses
Your existing events calendar on WordPress.
Setup
About 1 week (our estimate).
